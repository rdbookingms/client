Referral program settings

Clients see their own referral code on the main menu of the booking app. For every client they referred that is active on a paid plan they get the discount below on recurring plans, up to the maximum. Partners (people who are not clients) sign up on the partner page and earn a monthly share of confirmed recurring subscription payments for active clients. Quarterly and yearly payments accrue over their service months, within the term counted from the first payment. You pay partners yourself and log each payout here.

Lifetime has no earning cutoff while the referral stays active. One-time Lifetime plan purchases still earn no share.
Client discounts are recalculated from current eligibility; partner rates apply when each monthly credit is recorded. To exclude a specific client, open Clients → Edit → Exclude from referral program.

Prices

The price of each tier, and whether it is on offer. Clients see the paid plans and prices inside their app (subscription box, expiry reminder, lock screen) and in reminder emails. A tier with price 0, or one that is switched off, is not shown to clients. Prices also fill in the amount when you log a payment.

Normally 0 = free. Switched off = nobody can register by themselves.
Switching a tier off hides it from clients and stops you giving it to another client. Clients who already have it keep it and you can still renew them on it.

Resort limit and extra resorts

Every client can set up this many resorts by themselves. When they try to add more, the app sends you a request (you get an email, and the client shows "Wants +1 resort" in the Clients list). Nothing is added until you approve. Each approved extra resort adds the fee below for every month of the client's plan to their subscription total: with a fee of 50, one extra resort adds 50 to Monthly, 150 to Quarterly and 600 to Yearly. Lifetime clients owe it as a separate monthly amount.

Renewal contact info

How clients reach you to renew. Shown next to the prices. Leave a field empty to hide it. Changes reach every client within about 10 minutes.

Terms and Conditions for new clients

A new client must tick "I have read and accept the Terms and Conditions" before they can register. The link opens the text below, served by your Booking App at your app link?page=terms. Clients who registered earlier are not asked. The date a client accepted is kept in the Clients sheet (column "Terms Accepted").

Plain text. A line that starts with ## becomes a heading and **words** become bold. Saving sets "Last updated" to today.

Client activity thresholds

Based on the last successful direct client PIN login. Provider dashboard links, public calendars and subscription checks do not count. The gap after Actively using and before Rarely using is labeled Less active.

System email sender

Choose the account used for License Admin system and notification emails, including license keys, payment notices, referral messages, partner PIN codes and scheduled alerts.

To use another account, add and verify its address in the deploying account's Gmail settings under Accounts → Send mail as, then refresh the list below. Google handles the account connection; no email password is stored here. Updating this sender does not change who receives admin alerts.

Self-registration and the one-time Demo

When this is on, a new client can open the app, choose "Start a free 7-day demo" and be added here automatically with their own license key. Each email address gets one Demo, ever. You are emailed about every sign-up, and you can still give another Demo yourself (the "Allow another Demo" box).

Expiry alerts and automatic revoke

A subscription that reaches its end loses access by itself: the app locks at midnight on the expiry date and the client is marked "Revoked". You are emailed about expired clients within the hour, and every morning about clients that reached one of the marks below.

License Server URL

The web app URL of this project (Deploy > Manage deployments). Paste it here once so it is easy to copy. The same URL goes into LICENSE_SERVER_URL near the end of the booking app's Code.gs.

Payment QR code and instructions

Shown to clients with every renewal notice ("Pay now"): on the lock screen when a plan has ended, in the reminder banner, and under "Your subscription". Upload the QR code picture from your payment app (GCash, Maya, bank...). Clients scan it, pay the amount the app shows them, and upload a screenshot of the receipt. You then get an email and the receipt appears under Payments & finance.

The picture is resized to 420 pixels and kept in your Drive (folder "License Admin Files").
Clients see: "Receipts are checked within 48 hours. Need access right away? Contact us" with your renewal phone and email from the Pricing tab.

Booking App URL

The shared web app URL of the Booking App project. It is filled in by itself the first time a client uses the app - check that it is yours. Each client's "Copy login link" uses this address and still requires their Booking PIN. "Open dashboard" is your separate provider access without their PIN.
